## The idea

If you were to know the location, state and energy of every atom in the world, knowing every bit of data in the world, could you be able to predict the future? A philosopher named Laplace asked this very question, proposing a being called Laplace's demon. A further quesition that sprung from this is a question of if future activities are solely determined by current qualities, do you have free will over what happens next or are you only really following a 'script'. 

## A Widget against Free Will

Below I have inserted a small game of 'Conway's game of life', created by John Conway uses a 2D grid with select rules to create 'life'. This is also present in the banner of the website, always running. In short - if a cell is surrounded by 3 live cells it becomes alive and if a cell is alive and surrounded by 2 or 3 other cells it continues living. While using very basic rules to govern this game, the outcomes still vary wildly when 2 similar starting states are simulated. The point I am trying to convey is that maybe we don't have free will, real life is far more complex than conways game and therefore, if it is hard to predict  conways game it will be infinitely harder to predict real life, but perhaps possible. Maybe you are governed by the present and can't truly decide your future.

## A Rebuttle

Long after Laplace's time, we discovered the area of quantum mechanics and the niche aspects that make it extremely hard to predict. Due to the wave forms and randomness of the quantum aspects of the universe, predictibility may be impossible. Due to the high levels of chaos and randomness, people argue that current states do not fully influence the future state. Therefore, there may be an element of randomness that refutes the argument of determinism. 

## A Middle Ground

Another possibility is that while the universe may not be fully deterministic, it could be partially deterministic where current conditions create a probility distrubution of possible outcomes from the starting conditions. Therefore, we would not be fully governed by the past but heavily influenced. This is what I like to believe as I think it is extremely hard to truly beleive in determinism, letting the universe take you where ever 'you are meant to be'.
